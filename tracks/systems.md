# Systems engineering

How large systems store and move data, and what the machine does underneath the code.

- [~] **Designing Data-Intensive Applications, 2nd edition** by Martin Kleppmann and Chris Riccomini (Tier 2)
  The standard text on storage, replication, partitioning, and stream processing.
  Status: page 38 of about 600, chapter 2. Resumes after K&R and A Tour of C++.
- [ ] **Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective, 3rd edition** by Randal Bryant and David O'Hallaron (Tier 3)
  Machine-level code, linking, the memory hierarchy, virtual memory, and concurrency. Chapter 6 (caches and locality)
  is the follow-up to K&R's memory model: it explains why contiguous memory wins.
