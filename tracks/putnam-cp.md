# Putnam & competitive programming

## Reading

- [ ] **Competitive Programmer's Handbook** by Antti Laaksonen, draft of August 19, 2019 (Tier 2)
  Compact coverage of the standard CP toolkit: complexity, sorting, DP, graphs, number theory, geometry. Good fit for the 1000 to 1600 Codeforces range.
- [ ] **Putnam Guide** by R. Ebrahimian, University of Maryland, College Park (Tier 2)
  Topic-by-topic Putnam preparation with problem sets. Bridges AIME-style problem solving to written proofs.
