# Quant trading / research

Probability, statistics, and markets.

## Reading

- [~] **An Interview Primer for Quantitative Finance** by Dirk Bester, v1.2.0 (Tier 1)
  Worked probability and statistics questions with full solutions, plus regression theory and a set of standard tricks.
  Free PDF at github.com/dwcoder/QuantitativePrimer.
  Tracked as 58 items: 39 questions (the two R-specific ones are left out) and 19 reading sections. A question counts
  when it is solved and the answer checked; a section counts when read.
  Status: 2 of 58.
- [~] **A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews** by Xinfeng Zhou (Tier 2)
  Brain teasers, calculus and linear algebra, probability, stochastic processes, finance, and algorithms.
  Read by section as needed, not cover to cover. Per-problem status is in [greenbook-checklist.md](greenbook-checklist.md).
  Status: chapters 2 and 4 partly done, section 3.1 done.
- [ ] **An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Applications in Python** by Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie, Robert Tibshirani, and Jonathan Taylor (Tier 2)
  Applied statistics and machine learning foundations: regression, classification, resampling, tree methods, unsupervised learning.
- [ ] **The Theory of Poker** by David Sklansky (Tier 2)
  Expected value, pot odds, and decision-making under uncertainty.

## Probability and statistics

- [ ] **Introduction to Mathematical Statistics, 7th edition (2012)** by Robert V. Hogg, Joseph McKean, and Allen T. Craig (Tier 3)
  Proof-level probability and statistics. The theory underneath ISL.
- [ ] **Python for Data Analysis, 3rd edition** by Wes McKinney (Tier 3)
  pandas and NumPy from the pandas author. Practical tooling for research work.

## Markets

- [ ] **Trading and Exchanges: Market Microstructure for Practitioners** by Larry Harris (Tier 3)
  How markets work: order types, market makers, liquidity, price formation.
- [ ] **Paul Wilmott on Quantitative Finance** by Paul Wilmott (Tier 3)
  Broad, readable treatment of derivatives pricing and quant modeling. A reference more than a cover-to-cover read.

## Question banks

- [ ] **Quant Job Interview Questions and Answers** by Mark Joshi, Nick Denson, and Andrew Downes (Tier 3)
  Heavier on option pricing and stochastic calculus than Zhou.
- [ ] **Heard on the Street: Quantitative Questions from Wall Street Job Interviews** by Timothy Falcon Crack (Tier 3)
  Classic question bank. Overlaps with Zhou and Joshi, so skim for what is new.

## Courses

- **CMSC764 / AMSC764 Advanced Numerical Optimization** (University of Maryland, graduate)
  Convex analysis, gradient and quasi-Newton methods, duality, proximal and Lagrangian methods (ADMM, PDHG), and
  stochastic methods (SGD, MCMC), with implementations in Python. Applications in machine learning and model fitting.
- **MATH611 Combinatorics** (University of Maryland, graduate)
  Algebraic, enumerative, extremal, and probabilistic combinatorics, with an emphasis on proofs.
